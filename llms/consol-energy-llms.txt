# CONSOL Energy / Core Natural Resources, Inc.

> CONSOL Energy was a Pittsburgh-based bituminous coal producer. In January 2025
> it merged with Arch Resources to form Core Natural Resources, Inc. (NYSE: CNR);
> consolenergy.com now redirects to corenaturalresources.com. The company
> publishes no developer portal, no API documentation, no SDKs and no GitHub
> organization. It does serve the standard WordPress REST API anonymously from
> its corporate host, which is a real read-only content API over its mines,
> leadership, news releases, pages and published PDFs. Financial data is
> third-party, via the SEC's EDGAR APIs.
>
> Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-09-05 from probed endpoints and the
> provider's own route discovery document. Not published by the company.

## APIs

- [Mines Content API](https://corenaturalresources.com/wp-json/wp/v2/mine): 11 mine profiles (Bailey, Enlow Fork, Harvey, Beckley, Itmann, Mountain Laurel, Leer, Leer South, Black Thunder, West Elk, Coal Creek) plus the East/West mine-location taxonomy. Anonymous GET, HTTP 200.
- [Leadership Content API](https://corenaturalresources.com/wp-json/wp/v2/leader): 16 executive and board profiles with a leader-category taxonomy.
- [News Content API](https://corenaturalresources.com/wp-json/wp/v2/article): 40 news releases grouped into 9 series by the news-series-title taxonomy.
- [Site Content API](https://corenaturalresources.com/wp-json/wp/v2/pages): 22 corporate pages and a 173-item media library holding the supplier terms and conditions, Supplier Code of Conduct and sustainability/safety PDFs.
- [Discovery API](https://corenaturalresources.com/wp-json/): route index, registered types and taxonomies, post statuses, cross-type search (89 items) and oEmbed 1.0.
- [SEC EDGAR filings (third party)](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001710366.json): machine-readable filings for CIK 0001710366, published by the SEC, not by the company. Requires a declared User-Agent; 10 requests/second.

## Operating notes

- [Authentication](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/consol-energy/refs/heads/main/authentication/consol-energy-authentication.yml): none for reads; WordPress cookie+nonce or Application Passwords for writes, which the company does not issue.
- [Conventions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/consol-energy/refs/heads/main/conventions/consol-energy-conventions.yml): page/per_page pagination with X-WP-Total, X-WP-TotalPages and RFC 8288 Link headers; _fields sparse fieldsets; no request-id header.
- [Errors](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/consol-energy/refs/heads/main/errors/consol-energy-problem-types.yml): WordPress {code,message,data.status} envelope — NOT RFC 9457.
- [Rate limits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/consol-energy/refs/heads/main/rate-limits/consol-energy-rate-limits.yml): none published, none signalled in headers.
- [Lifecycle](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/api-evangelist/consol-energy/refs/heads/main/lifecycle/consol-energy-lifecycle.yml): no versioning policy, no deprecation policy, no status page. Treat the surface as unstable.
